# Luca Molluso

> Technical artist. An art director who builds what he directs.

I work in the middle: between the **idea** and the machines that make it real. I design the logic. Agents, tools and people run it. I hold the direction from the first line to the last frame.

==The outcome is cheap now. The idea isn't.==
