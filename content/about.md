TL;DR: I can't stop. The rest is evidence.

I started as a modder. Years of game mods got me hired at IBM in 2017, no joke. I've shipped Android apps, web platforms and real-time 3D experiences ever since, now at Accenture Song. Eventually, writing the code stopped being enough. I wanted to decide how things should look and feel, so I went back to school without letting my job slip. A day-and-night grind, and an Art Direction degree in 2025.

## Where my work lives

- **Real-time 3D.** Product configurators (five and counting) and digital twins for luxury fashion and automotive brands, with Babylon.js, Unreal Engine and Blender, plus an in-house framework so nobody builds the same thing twice by hand.
- **Agentic AI.** Workflows where agents do the heavy lifting and a human eye makes the call: Claude Code, MCP servers, local models, ComfyUI. I teach GenAI to classes of about 50 in the Palestre Digitali program. AI is a tool, not a goal.
- **Art direction.** Concept, mood, type, colour, and knowing when to say no.

## The rest of me

Ten years of transverse flute at the Santa Cecilia Conservatory, where I learned that taste is something you practise every day. Five years streaming on Twitch, from 2019 to 2024. Games are a long-standing crush: I play half for fun, half to work out how they were built. Linux, OBS, Blender: FOSS whenever it does the job.

Most of my client work is under NDA, so the best stories happen over a call. If you're building worlds, products or pipelines and need someone who speaks artist and engineer, write me.
